# Complex Analysis Visualizer in Python

A collection of 14 laboratory projects exploring applied complex analysis, mathematical geometry, and algorithm visualization in the complex plane.

## Core Mathematical Concepts Implemented

Throughout the `AC_lab` projects, various mathematical cases are modeled and rendered:
* **Power Series & Approximations:** Visualizing Exponential functions and Taylor Series expansion limits.
* **Rational Functions:** Evaluating and expanding polynomial fractions and tracking arguments/modulus in the complex plane.
* **Geometric & Trigonometric Forms:** Drawing Archimedean spirals, dynamic geometric series, and procedural polygonal tessellations (e.g., Decagon/Pentagon recursive drawing).
* **Root Trajectories:** Calculating and rendering complex roots and power transformations dynamically over time.

(The mathematical algorithms and logic in these scripts were independently implemented based on course requirements. The graphical rendering logic and window management rely on a custom Pygame wrapper (`ComplexPygame` & `Color`) provided by the university course instructor for educational purposes.)
