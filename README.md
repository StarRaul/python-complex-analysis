# Complex Analysis Visualizer in Python

A collection of 14 laboratory projects exploring applied complex analysis, mathematical geometry, and algorithm visualization in the complex plane.
## Included Projects

### 1) AC_lab_01
Elementary transcendental series & complex square root
### 2) AC_lab_02
2D parametric curves, roulettes & logarithmic/Archimedean spirals
### 3) AC_lab_03
Affine planar motions, astroid hypocycloid, kaleidoscope & disk regions
### 4) AC_lab_04
Discrete orbits, Jordan curve winding numbers & computational geometry
### 5) AC_lab_05
Conformal power grids, Fermat–Torricelli point & polygon whirls
### 6) AC_lab_06
Contour integration, Newton–Leibniz, Euler line, Feuerbach circle & Van Aubel
### 7) AC_lab_07
Cauchy's integral formula, barycentric morphing & dynamic triangle center trajectories
### 8) AC_lab_08
Power/rational Taylor expansions, star polygons & decagon-pentagon rosettes
### 9) AC_lab_09
Cauchy residue theorem, 26-gon rhombus tiles, golden ratio & Padovan spirals
### 10) AC_lab_10
Curve fractals (Koch, Lévy, Dragon), L-systems, Pythagoras tree & wind-swayed trees
### 11) AC_lab_11
Space-filling curves (Morton Z, Hilbert, Peano) & 2-map IFS fractal attractors
### 12) AC_lab_12
Newton-Raphson fractal basins and Sierpiński carpet constructions
### 13) AC_lab_13
Julia set fractals, inverse iteration dynamics, and parallelogram homotopies
### 14) AC_lab_14
Mandelbrot-Julia correspondence, cobweb orbits, and Feigenbaum bifurcation diagram

(SOME of the mathematical algorithms and logic in these scripts were independently implemented based on course requirements. The graphical rendering logic and window management rely on a custom Pygame wrapper (`ComplexPygame` & `Color`) provided by the university course instructor for educational purposes.)
